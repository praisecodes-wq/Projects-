Schools Pilot New Reading Program This Fall

Grade 11 and 12 students have been working towards getting their private pilot's licenses, spending the end of every school day at Summit Air to learn all about what it takes to be a pilot. Now, they've got their flight jackets to prove it. From left to right in the back are students Nicholas Graham, Marcus Vandermeer, Joseph Depew and Nolan Dusome. From left to right in the front are associate principal Cindy Kimove, and students Corbi Roy, Leander Andrew Dun and Holly Murray. (Liny Lamberink/CBC)

Leander Dun has always wanted to fly.

He wants to fly so much that he and his mom moved to Yellowknife so he could take part in a new aviation program at École St. Patrick High School.

He's earning his stripes — plus a new jacket.

“It feels amazing,” he said. “This has been my dream ever since I was a little kid.”
